Last updated: November 27, 2026

Malfoid Stickers is developed by Sergio Daniel Xalambri (“I,” “me,” or “my”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use Malfoid Stickers on iPhone and iPad, both the companion app and the sticker browser inside Messages, and, separately, when you contact Malfoid Stickers support through this website.

## The app collects no data

Malfoid Stickers does not collect or share any personal information. It has no account and no sign-in, and it does not send anything to servers operated by me or anyone else. The only thing it keeps is the list of stickers you mark as favorites, stored on your device as described below.

The app includes no advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting SDKs or third-party code. It is completely free and offers no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

## How the app works

Malfoid Stickers has two parts:

- **The companion app**, where you can browse, search and preview the stickers, mark favorites, and copy a sticker’s image to paste it into another app.
- **The sticker browser in Messages**, where you can browse, search and mark favorites, and insert a sticker into a conversation or drag it onto a message.

Search and filtering happen on your device. What you search for and which stickers you favorite are never sent to me or to anyone else.

All sticker artwork is bundled with the app and loads from your device, so it works offline and the app makes no network requests to show it.

## Favorites stored on your device

When you mark a sticker as a favorite, the app saves that sticker’s identifier on your device using Apple’s standard app preferences (UserDefaults). Only the identifiers of the stickers you choose are stored, nothing else. The companion app and the sticker browser in Messages each keep their own list of favorites.

These preferences stay on your device. They are not sent to me or to any server, and I have no way to see them. They are app settings, not personal data collected about you.

## Copying stickers to the clipboard

When you tap a sticker in the companion app, it copies that sticker’s image, a PNG with a transparent background, to the system clipboard so you can paste it into another app.

The app copies only when you tap a sticker, and it never reads what is on your clipboard. Once an image is on the clipboard, the system clipboard and the app you paste it into handle it under their own behavior and privacy practices.

## What the app can access

Malfoid Stickers does not access your conversations, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location, and it does not ask for permission to use any of them.

## Messages and Apple

When you send a sticker, Messages delivers it like any other part of your conversation, under Apple’s policies and your device settings. Malfoid Stickers is not involved in that delivery and cannot see what you send or to whom.

Apple distributes Malfoid Stickers through the App Store and may process download, diagnostics and usage information under its own privacy policy and your device settings. Apple may make aggregate analytics or diagnostic reports available to me through its developer services. I use any such information to understand app performance and resolve problems.

For details about Apple’s practices, visit [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Deleting the app

Deleting Malfoid Stickers removes it from your device together with the preferences it stored there, including both lists of favorites. Stickers you already sent stay in your conversations, like any other message, and images you already pasted into other apps stay where you pasted them.

## Support requests through this website

The support form is part of this website, not the app. Nothing you do in the app is sent to me; I receive only what you choose to submit through the form, which may include:

- Your name, if provided.
- Your email address.
- Your request topic and device.
- Your iOS or iPadOS and app versions, if provided.
- Your message and any other information you choose to include.

I use this information to respond to your request, investigate reported problems, and improve Malfoid Stickers. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

## Support website and service providers

The support website is hosted on Cloudflare, which also delivers support requests to me by email. Cloudflare processes the technical information needed to serve and protect the website, such as your IP address and browser details, under [Cloudflare’s privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

To limit spam, the support form includes hidden fields that automated tools tend to fill in, and it limits how many requests one IP address can send per minute. Each request is also checked automatically for signs of spam, such as many links or a disposable email address; a request that looks like spam may be discarded or marked for review. Your IP address is not included in the support request I receive.

As part of that check, your IP address, email address, and name, if provided, are looked up in [StopForumSpam](https://www.stopforumspam.com/), a public database of addresses reported for sending spam. StopForumSpam receives only those details, not your message, and handles them under [its privacy policy](https://www.stopforumspam.com/privacy).

The website keeps request logs through Cloudflare to diagnose problems. They record details of each request, such as the page requested and the outcome, and do not include the contents of your support request.

The website uses [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to measure visits, such as which pages are viewed and where visitors come from. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies or local storage and does not track you across websites. The website does not use advertising cookies.

After you submit the form, the website sets one cookie, `r3:session`, so the next page can confirm that your request was sent; that cookie expires after one year.

Support information is not sold or used for targeted advertising. Service providers may process it as necessary to provide hosting, email delivery, or security services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

## Retention

The app keeps no data about you beyond the favorites stored on your device, which it never sends anywhere and which deleting the app removes. Support requests are kept in my email inbox until the issue or request is resolved, and then deleted.

## Children

Malfoid Stickers does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The support form asks only for what is needed to reply.
