---
title: Modular scale
description: A sequence of sizes where each step is the previous one multiplied by a fixed ratio, so every size in a design relates to every other by the same proportion.
kind: glossary
date: "2026-03-03"
tags: [typography, tokens]
---

Start from a base, usually the body text size, and multiply by the ratio to go up a step or divide to go down. With a 1rem base and a ratio of 1.25 the steps are 0.8rem, 1rem, 1.25rem, 1.5625rem and so on. Ratios are often named after musical intervals: 1.2 is a minor third, 1.25 a major third, 1.333 a perfect fourth.

A larger ratio gives more contrast between headings and body, which wide screens can afford and phones usually cannot. That is why my own scale changes its ratio with the viewport, as described in [A type scale you can explain in one sentence](/showcase/plinth/demo/articles/type-scale-in-one-sentence).
