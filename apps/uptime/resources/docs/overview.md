Uptime continuously monitors your infrastructure and notifies you the moment an issue is detected. Whether you're running a simple website or a complex distributed system, Uptime gives you the visibility you need to maintain high availability.

## Key Features

### Flexible Monitoring Options

- **HTTP Monitors** - Check website and API availability, response times, and content
- **DNS Monitors** - Watch a domain's DNS records for unexpected changes
- **TCP Monitors** - Monitor server ports and services

### Cron Job Monitoring

Track your scheduled tasks and background jobs. Get notified if a job fails to run on time or reports an error.

### Alerts

Receive notifications through multiple channels when monitors detect issues. Configure alert conditions and escalation policies to match your team's workflow.

### Status Pages

Create public or private status pages to communicate service health with your users. Keep stakeholders informed during incidents and scheduled maintenance.

### Maintenance Windows

Schedule maintenance periods to pause monitoring and prevent false alerts during planned downtime.
